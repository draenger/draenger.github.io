About

Senior Software Developer based in Silesia, Poland. About five years of commercial work, the last three in AI/ML.

Day job: enterprise AI assistants and RAG systems on Azure. I work in .NET and Python with Semantic Kernel and Azure AI Search, and I wire in OpenTelemetry tracing so every answer can be traced back to the retrieval and model calls behind it.

After hours: I build and ship my own products (Zryw, Whisperio, rezme, Jarvis) with an AI agent setup that does much of the implementation while I review and test.
